## Pricing models that fit

- **Audit → retainer ladder** (best for solo): low-friction entry, converts
  on evidence. Audit priced to be a no-brainer; retainer priced on value.
- **Per-location** (local): clean math for multi-location clients
  (e.g., $X/location/mo). Mirrors how they already buy reputation/SEO tools.
- **AEO/GEO add-on to SEO retainer** (+$1,500–6,000/mo): if you or a partner
  already sell SEO. Fastest sale — the client relationship exists.
- Avoid: pure performance pricing (you don't control the engines; citation
  volatility is 33–50% run-to-run) and hourly for delivery (caps your upside).

## Starter recommendation (solo founder, local niche)

- **Audit: $750 one-time** (first 5 at $500 as founding-client pricing).
  Undercuts the $2k+ market while you build proof; takes you ~6–8 hours once
  the checklist is productized. Raise to $1,500 after 5 published case
  snippets.
- **Local AI Presence retainer: $900/mo** per location, 3-month minimum.
  Below the $1,500 "real GEO" floor cited by UK agencies — justified because
  local delivery is narrower (GBP + reviews + listings + 2 pages/mo) and your
  cost base is ~$30/mo tooling + your time.
- **Citation Engine: $2,500/mo**, 6-month minimum. Priced against the
  $2,000–5,000 SMB band; only offered once you can show one audit-to-retainer
  win.

Why: you enter under the market to buy proof, not to stay cheap. Every price
above has a named raise trigger (5 audits → $1,500; 3 retainer wins →
$1,200/mo; waitlist → $3,500 for Citation Engine). Cheap without a raise
plan is just cheap.
