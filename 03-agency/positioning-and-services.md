**share of voice / mention rate**; the credible ones show dated prompt sets
and per-engine numbers; the weak ones show only percentages with no base.

## Where the gap is for a solo founder

Incumbents chase SaaS and enterprise ($5k–25k/mo). Almost nobody sells
**local GEO** to restaurants, salons, clinics, and trades — even though local
is the segment where the playbook is most concrete (GBP, reviews, listings)
and the buyer's pain is simplest ("ChatGPT doesn't recommend me").
A solo founder's edge: niche down (one vertical, one metro), sell the audit,
productize the monthly.

## 3 starter packages

### Package A — AI Visibility Audit (entry, one-time)

- Manual + tool-based prompt panel (25 prompts × 3 engines): where the client
  appears, who beats them, which sources feed the answers.
- Crawler-access check (robots.txt), GBP/listings completeness, review audit,
  content structure review, schema gaps.
- Deliverable: 10–15 page report + 90-day prioritized fix list + 30-min
  walkthrough. No implementation.
- Why it sells: low commitment, creates the implementation pipeline, and the
  live-demo moment ("here's what ChatGPT says about your competitor") closes.

### Package B — Local AI Presence (monthly, the core retainer)

For one location. Monthly: GBP optimization + posts, review-generation
system (ask scripts, QR cards, reply handling), NAP consistency cleanup,
1–2 answer-first content pieces (service pages, FAQs), listing management
(Bing/Yelp/Apple), monthly prompt-panel report.
- Why it sells: recurring, tangible, and local businesses already buy
  adjacent services (SEO, reputation management).

### Package C — Citation Engine (monthly, higher tier)

For multi-location or contested categories. Everything in B, plus: digital PR
outreach targeting publications the engines cite, Reddit/community presence
program (genuine participation, never spam), comparison/listicle placement
outreach, quarterly content refresh cycles, competitor displacement tracking.
- Why it sells: this is where the earned-media citation data says the real
  leverage is — and it's the work clients can't do themselves.

Start with A to get cash flowing and proof assets; convert 20–30% of audits
to B; offer C only when you have PR capacity (even outsourced).
