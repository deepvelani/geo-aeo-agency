# Outreach & Sales: How GEO Agencies Get Clients

## Channels that work in this niche

1. **Live-demo outreach** (highest converting). The standard agency pitch,
   per LLM Pulse's agency guide: ask "Do you know what ChatGPT says about
   your brand?" then show them — live. When they see a competitor mentioned
   and they aren't, the conversation flips from "why should I care?" to "how
   fast can we start?" ([llmpulse.ai](https://llmpulse.ai/blog/geo-agency-guide/))
2. **Local walk-in / call with a one-page audit teaser.** For the local
   niche: run 5 prompts before you visit, print the one-pager ("ChatGPT
   recommends your 3 competitors, not you — here's the 5-line reason").
   Tangible, specific, hard to ignore.
3. **Content that demonstrates the craft.** Publish your own prompt-panel
   studies ("We tested 50 prompts for Toronto dentists — here's who ChatGPT
   recommends and why"). This is both marketing and proof of method.
4. **Partnerships with SEO/web agencies.** They have the client relationships;
   you white-label the GEO add-on (+$1,500–6,000/mo market band). One partner
   sending 2 audits/month beats 100 cold emails.
5. **Communities.** r/SEO, local business groups, indie-hacker channels —
   answer questions genuinely (the r/marketing crowd publicly shames
   disguised promotion; don't be that brand — [typescape.ai](https://typescape.ai/blog/what-is-geo)).

## Pitch angles (pick one per prospect)

- **The invisibility gap:** "You rank #1 on Google but ChatGPT recommends
  your competitor." (Real phenomenon — Maps presence ≠ ChatGPT recommendation,
  [makkdigital](https://makkdigital.com/en/blog/how-chatgpt-recommends-local-businesses).)
- **The review angle:** "AI answers summarize your reviews. Your last 10
  reviews decide what ChatGPT tells customers." (True — AI answers routinely
  summarize review text.)
- **The compounding angle:** citation share is concentrating (15 domains = 68%
  of all citations); early presence compounds as AI referral share grows
  toward a projected ~5% of organic sessions.

## Proof assets to build (in order)

1. **Before/after prompt tests** — dated screenshots, 3 runs per prompt,
   same prompt set. The case-study template from LLM Pulse's agency guide:
   baseline (SoM, mentions, sentiment) → actions (pages restructured, schema,
   crawl fixes, new content) → 90-day results. Example skeleton they suggest:
   SoM 4% → 18%, mentions +340%, citations +280%. **Use your real numbers —
   never fabricate; the template's numbers are illustrative.**
   ([llmpulse.ai](https://llmpulse.ai/blog/geo-agency-guide/))
2. **One vertical study** — 30–50 prompts in one niche, published as a blog
   post. Doubles as content marketing.
3. **Client logos + one-line results** — even "mentioned in 0/25 prompts →
   9/25 in 90 days" is a story.

Reference real agency proof for format (not to copy numbers): uSERP's SaaS
case (AI Mode visibility 66.6% → 92.0%, ChatGPT mention rate 56.7%,
[case study](https://userp.io/ai-seo-agency/)); GR0's ticketing case (revenue
+843% alongside LLM traffic growth, [case study](https://gr0.com/blog/gr0-geo-results)).
Both are vendor-published — cite as their claims, not as benchmarks.

## Sample outreach script (cold email / DM)

> Subject: what ChatGPT says about [Business Name]
>
> Hi [Name] —
>
> I asked ChatGPT "best [service] in [city]" three times this week. [Competitor
> A] and [Competitor B] got recommended every time. [Business] didn't come up
> once — even though you have [X] Google reviews and rank well on Maps.
>
> The reason is specific: [one concrete finding, e.g. "your robots.txt blocks
> OAI-SearchBot" / "ChatGPT pulls from Yelp here and your listing is
> unclaimed" / "reviewers never mention [service], so the AI has nothing to
> quote"].
>
> I put together a one-page breakdown of exactly where you stand across
> ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini, and the 5 fixes that would change it. Happy
> to send it over — no pitch attached.
>
> — [Your name]

Rules: one concrete finding per message (proves you did the work), no
guarantees (there is no paid placement in ChatGPT — saying otherwise kills
trust, [8sonics.com](https://8sonics.com/how-chatgpt-recommends-local-businesses/)),
and the asset (one-pager) does the selling so the email stays short.
