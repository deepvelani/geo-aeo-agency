# Local Business Playbook: Getting Recommended by AI Assistants

For a restaurant, salon, clinic, or trades business. Ordered by impact —
do items 1–5 before anything else.

## 1. Fix crawler access (15 minutes, free)

AI engines can't cite what they can't read. In `robots.txt`, explicitly allow
`OAI-SearchBot` (ChatGPT), `Googlebot`, `Bingbot`, `PerplexityBot`, and
`Claude-SearchBot`. OpenAI says changes register in ~24 hours. Do NOT assume
blocking AI crawlers "protects" your content — for a local business it only
protects you from being recommended.
([madrobot.blog](https://madrobot.blog/2026/10/06/how-to-get-chatgpt-ai-to-recommend-your-business/),
[8sonics.com](https://8sonics.com/how-chatgpt-recommends-local-businesses/))

## 2. Complete the Google Business Profile (1–2 hours, free)

For "near me" questions, the GBP is the single biggest lever: AI assistants
lean heavily on Google Maps and Business Profile data when recommending local
businesses. Fill everything: the most specific primary category possible,
secondary categories, every service, exact hours, phone, website, service
areas, 10+ real photos, and regular posts (offers, news, photos).
([digilago.ma](https://www.digilago.ma/en/guide-google-chatgpt-maroc.html),
[heplink.co.uk](https://heplink.co.uk/insights/how-to-get-your-business-recommended-by-chatgpt))

Reality check: showing up in Google Maps ≠ showing up in ChatGPT. One agency
study found businesses appearing in 2/3 of Maps searches were named in only
1/3 of ChatGPT answers. Maps is necessary, not sufficient. ([makkdigital](https://makkdigital.com/en/blog/how-chatgpt-recommends-local-businesses))

## 3. Reviews: volume, velocity, and keywords in the text (ongoing)

Reviews feed the "popularity" signal in local search, and AI answers routinely
summarize what reviewers say. Ask happy customers to mention **the service and
the city** in their words ("best facial in Mississauga" beats "great place").
Reply to every review — it signals an actively managed profile. Never pay for
or fake reviews; Google's guidance says inauthentic mentions help less than
people think and count as spam.
([8sonics.com](https://8sonics.com/how-chatgpt-recommends-local-businesses/),
[heplink.co.uk](https://heplink.co.uk/insights/how-to-get-your-business-recommended-by-chatgpt))
A practical bar used by practitioners: 20+ recent Google reviews before
expecting AI visibility. ([heplink.co.uk](https://heplink.co.uk/insights/how-to-get-your-business-recommended-by-chatgpt))

## 4. Claim the other listings ChatGPT actually reads (1 hour, free)

- **Bing Places** — free, takes minutes; Bing is widely believed to feed ChatGPT
  search results. ([8sonics.com](https://8sonics.com/how-chatgpt-recommends-local-businesses/))
- **Yelp** — Yelp's data now flows into ChatGPT. Add services, hours, photos,
  service area. ([8sonics.com](https://8sonics.com/how-chatgpt-recommends-local-businesses/))
- **Apple Business Connect** — Apple Maps coverage. ([madrobot.blog](https://madrobot.blog/2026/10/06/how-to-get-chatgpt-ai-to-recommend-your-business/))

Keep name, address, phone, and hours **identical everywhere**. Inconsistent
NAP is how a business becomes "unresolvable" to an entity-matching model.

## 5. One clear page per service (copy work, free)

Write in plain customer words: what you do, for whom, where, and roughly what
it costs — in the first two sentences. One page per service (and per city, if
multi-location). A page on "emergency plumber in Toronto" matches a spoken
question in a way a generic services page can't. Add a short FAQ per page.
([madrobot.blog](https://madrobot.blog/2026/10/06/how-to-get-chatgpt-ai-to-recommend-your-business/))

## 6. Schema markup (1 hour, free)

`LocalBusiness` (or the specific subtype: `Restaurant`, `Dentist`,
`BeautySalon`) + `FAQPage` on service pages. Google's May 2026 guide says
schema isn't required for AI Overviews citation — but it costs nothing, helps
machines read your facts, and Perplexity/ChatGPT-side practitioners still
recommend it. Harmless upside. ([digilago.ma](https://www.digilago.ma/en/guide-google-chatgpt-maroc.html))

## 7. Earn one local mention per quarter (ongoing)

Chamber of commerce, sponsor a local event, pitch a local "best of" list, get
in a city guide. ChatGPT's local answers are built from the sources that point
to you — reviews, directories, local articles — not from your website alone.
([8sonics.com](https://8sonics.com/how-chatgpt-recommends-local-businesses/),
[madrobot.blog](https://madrobot.blog/2026/10/06/how-to-get-chatgpt-ai-to-recommend-your-business/))

## 8. Set up measurement before you start

- Google Search Console → Generative AI performance report (shows how people
  find you through AI Overviews and AI Mode). Free.
  ([madrobot.blog](https://madrobot.blog/2026/10/06/how-to-get-chatgpt-ai-to-recommend-your-business/))
- Monthly manual prompt test: ask ChatGPT "best [service] in [city]" in a
  private window, record who gets named and which sites are cited. This is your
  baseline. ([heplink.co.uk](https://heplink.co.uk/insights/how-to-get-your-business-recommended-by-chatgpt))

## What NOT to promise a local client

There is **no paid placement or business directory inside ChatGPT's local
answers** — businesses get named because the sources ChatGPT reads point to
them. Anyone guaranteeing a spot is lying. The only reliable path is earning
trust across the web.
([8sonics.com](https://8sonics.com/how-chatgpt-recommends-local-businesses/))
