## ⭐ Start here

**"What Is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)? A Researcher's Definition"**
— fernandoangulo.com
https://fernandoangulo.com/blog/what-is-generative-engine-optimization

Why: the single best one-page foundation — defines GEO as retrieve → trust →
cite, anchors everything to the Princeton paper, and gives per-engine
citation-slot counts (~3 on Gemini to ~15 on ChatGPT, Semrush AI Visibility
Index 2026). Updated days before this repo was written.

2. **Contently — "What Is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?"**
   https://contently.com/2026/04/29/what-is-generative-engine-optimization-geo/

   strategy, co-citation with .gov/.edu).

   https://typescape.ai/blog/what-is-geo

   https://madrobot.blog/2026/10/06/how-to-get-chatgpt-ai-to-recommend-your-business/

   https://llmpulse.ai/blog/geo-agency-guide/
