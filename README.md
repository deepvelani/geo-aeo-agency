# GEO/AEO Knowledge Base

A public learning + agency-launch knowledge base for **AI-search visibility** —
getting brands recommended and cited inside AI-generated answers from
ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Copilot.

This is a working document, not a finished book. Every substantive claim is
linked to its source. Claims marked **[vendor]** come from company marketing;
claims marked **[independent]** come from peer-reviewed papers or third-party
studies. Vendor numbers are directionally useful and statistically suspect —
treat them that way.

> Last updated: 2026-10-17. This field moves fast. Citation patterns shift in
> weeks (one study saw ChatGPT's Reddit share fall ~60% → ~10% in six weeks
> after a single parameter change — [5WPR](https://completeaitraining.com/news/5wpr-index-finds-reddit-accounts-for-40-of-ai-citations/)),
> so verify any 2025-era claim before building on it.

## 4-week learning roadmap

**Week 1 — Foundations.** Read
[`01-field/what-is-geo-aeo.md`](01-field/what-is-geo-aeo.md) and the Princeton
GEO paper summary in [`01-field/key-studies.md`](01-field/key-studies.md).
Understand retrieve → trust → cite, and why ~80% of what moves AI citation is
ordinary SEO fundamentals ([coworkers.global](https://www.coworkers.global/blog/what-is-generative-engine-optimization)).

**Week 2 — Mechanics.** Read
[`01-field/how-ai-search-ranks.md`](01-field/how-ai-search-ranks.md). Learn the
per-engine citation patterns (Reddit everywhere, Wikipedia on ChatGPT, Google
properties on AI Overviews, research sources on Perplexity). Start a manual
prompt panel for one niche: 15 questions × 3 engines, weekly.

**Week 3 — Delivery.** Work through
[`02-tactics/local-business-playbook.md`](02-tactics/local-business-playbook.md)
and [`02-tactics/content-and-citations.md`](02-tactics/content-and-citations.md).
Run a full audit on a real business (yours, a friend's) using the checklist in
[`03-agency/operations-sops.md`](03-agency/operations-sops.md).

**Week 4 — Agency.** Read the `03-agency/` folder end to end. Pick one service
package from [`positioning-and-services.md`](03-agency/positioning-and-services.md),
price it from [`pricing.md`](03-agency/pricing.md), and do 10 live-demo
outreaches using the script in
[`outreach-and-sales.md`](03-agency/outreach-and-sales.md).

Outcome at week 4: you can run an AI-visibility audit, explain exactly what a
client is missing, and report share-of-voice over time. That's a sellable service.
